How do recommendations work?

Three main candidates, plus an evidence-based trial pick. Main picks require at least 20 personal games and no clear performance risk. The discovery slot looks for an unplayed or rarely played hero using current rank win-rate data, your optional aim preference and shared kit skills. This is a learning hypothesis, not a guarantee. Ten wins in ten games receive at most 9.1% personal-result weight and are insufficient for a main slot. Poorly performing heroes appear separately under Improve first; they do not occupy the discovery slot.

Results, experience and rank win rates. Competitive and Quick Play are combined. The population context blends 70% current role rank and 30% target rank. Win rate is central. Below-median pick share within the same role tempers positive win-rate deviations by up to 25%; it never conceals a poor win rate. Above-median pick share adds at most 0.75 points and bans at most 0.5, reduced for win rates below 52% and absent at 48% or lower. Missing context is neutral. These are bounded coaching heuristics, not statistical confidence or an official tier list. Evidence is the minimum of hero games and minutes played / 10. Personal-result weight is min(evidence, 400) / (min(evidence, 400) + 100): even with abundant data, at least 20% of that baseline remains current meta weight. Hours provide up to 3 familiarity points, reduced for negative personal results. An uncertainty deduction moderates small samples. The index is not a predicted win rate.

Hours do not rescue poor performance. A DPS hero with fewer than one elimination per death, at least 20 deaths and either an hour or 10 games cannot become a main or trial pick. Eliminations include assists; this DPS threshold is not applied to support or tank. For all heroes, persistently poor win results are compared with the same hero in rank win-rate data, moderated for small samples. At 20 games or more, below 45% wins and more than 5 percentage points below hero meta, improvement comes first. Clear underperformance is screened before aim and hours bonuses. These are conservative coaching rules, not official rank standards.

Less aim. Current heroes are assessed across their full kits: targeted healing, melee, spread and explosions versus tracking, precise hits and projectile prediction. Preferences range from −4 to +6 points and apply only while the mode is enabled. Positive bonuses are reduced for poor personal results. Low aim requirements do not imply easy positioning, timing or movement. Each hero's assessment appears under Playstyle and aim requirements.

New heroes and reworks. The catalogue and ability descriptions refresh online. Reviewed assessments are tied to the exact set of ability descriptions, not just the hero name. Changed descriptions or an age of 90 days trigger a limited primary-weapon analysis instead. Numerical balance changes without text changes are not detected as mechanical changes; current meta remains important for those. There are no fixed hero-to-hero recommendations.

Discovery. Specific weapon tasks carry more weight than generic shared movement. A source hero must be in your current top 3 for a role. A positive source requires at least 30 games and two hours, at least 5% of role playtime and 25% of the hours on your most-played role hero, wins above its loaded rank win-rate data and no clear performance risk. Ignored heroes cannot be source heroes. At least two sufficiently specific shared traits must support transfer. The signal is capped at 5 points and halved across roles. Without a supported match to your top 3, no arbitrary flex hero appears. The aim toggle affects ordering but is not evidence of transferable skill by itself. An unproven hero does not displace a proven main.

Seasons and freshness. Public meta is fetched again on refresh. The API does not provide a selectable season or reliable patch ID with these figures: fetched today does not guarantee coverage of the latest patch. Blizzard warns updates may lag by up to a day. Local meta older than 24 hours is flagged; after 72 hours it no longer ranks heroes. Known season changes, source changes and decreasing counters break recent-form comparisons. Career stats may include old seasons and kits; exact game dates are unavailable.

What is compared with other players? Hero win rates at the loaded role rank and target rank, platform and region. This HTML fetches a shared pool of public profiles directly and calculates the mean of player averages for each hero and current role rank. Competitive and Quick Play are combined on both sides, weighted by hero playtime. Each eligible player counts once. Averages are shown from five players; from ten, a supported adjustment may contribute up to two points. These are not official population standards or percentiles. Good healing does not automatically mean sufficient offensive contribution; high damage may come from farming tanks. Mitigation uses actual mitigation fields only, never silently substituting damage blocked.

Recent form. Up to 120 local snapshots over 14 days. From five comparable additional hero games, recent form can contribute up to ±3 points. These are changes in career counters, not individual match logs. They may change recommendations, but ten new games do not guarantee a different order.

Controls. Both modes start disabled. The aim toggle works while loading. × hides a hero for that profile; Restore all suggestions removes its manual exclusions. Performance checks still apply. Target rank is automatically one rank higher, or Masters when rank is unknown.
